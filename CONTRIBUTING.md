# Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome. Describe what you expected, what happened,
your operating system and browser, and the steps needed to reproduce the issue.
Use generated or public sample files. Do not attach private documents, videos,
recordings, subtitles, access tokens, or personal logs.

Keep changes focused and include the checks you ran. For user-facing changes,
update the usage guide and verify the affected workflow. Preserve local processing,
accessible labels, cancellation, and the distinction between original files and exports.
New dependencies must include their version, purpose, and license information.
